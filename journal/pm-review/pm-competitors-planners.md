# TripTab competitor research: trip planners

Scope: the trip-planning side. Wanderlog (required), TripIt, Lambus, plus short notes on Troupe and Mindtrip.
Researched 2026-11-23 with web search and page fetches. Every claim below links to a page that was opened in this session, except where marked.

## 1. Wanderlog

### Group collaboration
- Invite by email or by sharing a link; tripmates edit in real time. App description: "add your tripmates with their email address or by sharing a link to the itinerary." https://apps.apple.com/app/id1476732439
- Two share levels, "Can edit" and "Can view"; trip visibility can be Public, Friends (followers) or Private. Wanderlog says it will never limit "the number of people you can plan with." https://wanderlog.com/blog/faq
- Wanderlog's own co-founder post (Oct 14, 2024) says collaboration is free and invited friends can add suggestions or edit. https://wanderlog.com/blog/?p=1120
- Does an invitee need an account? Not stated on the pages opened. The FAQ says you can start planning on the web without an account (https://wanderlog.com/blog/faq), while a Jan 2026 Trustpilot review says "Cannot plan trip unless you sign up" (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/wanderlog.com). Reviewers describe friends being asked to log in (see complaints).
- Voting: no voting or polls feature appeared on any Wanderlog page opened. From memory, unverified: Wanderlog has no structured voting. (A search summary credited Wanderlog with polls, but the PopSci article it cited gives polls to Howbout, not Wanderlog: https://www.popsci.com/diy/group-trip-planning-apps/.)

### Bookings import
- Free: forward confirmation emails into the trip. https://apps.apple.com/app/id1476732439
- Pro: link Gmail so Wanderlog scans for flight and hotel confirmations ("parses your Gmail inbox only for flight and hotel confirmations"). https://wanderlog.com/blog/faq and https://wanderlog.com/blog/?p=1120

### Costs, budgets, splitting
- App description: "Set a budget for yourself or a group," split bills, record who paid and who owes. https://apps.apple.com/app/id1476732439
- Co-founder post: splitting is free, in an Expenses tab, with settle-up at the end. https://wanderlog.com/blog/?p=1120
- Its own FAQ still says "We currently don't have a great way to track expenses" (likely stale, but it is live on the site). https://wanderlog.com/blog/faq
- Reviewers call splitting "functional but basic." https://localsinsider.com/apps/wanderlog-review-free-offer-collaborative-travel-planning-done-right/

### Pricing (vendor sources)
- Pro is $39.99 a year per the co-founder's post. https://wanderlog.com/blog/?p=1120
- The US App Store lists many Pro price points: annual plans at $31.99, $39.99, $49.99 and $59.99, and monthly plans at $5.99 and $16.99. https://apps.apple.com/app/id1476732439
- Pro adds offline maps, Gmail scanning, Google Maps export and route optimization (https://wanderlog.com/blog/?p=1120), plus, per one review site, unlimited AI and attachments, dark mode and no ads (https://localsinsider.com/apps/wanderlog-review-free-offer-collaborative-travel-planning-done-right/). The free tier caps AI at 5 messages a trip, per https://travelvient.com/guides/best-group-travel-planning-apps/.

### Top praises
1. Everything in one place, replacing spreadsheets and several apps; plan on a computer and have it "saved there for when I'm on the road." https://apps.apple.com/us/app/wanderlog-travel-planner/id1476732439?see-all=reviews
2. Good for planning with friends while apart: a Nov 2026 reviewer planned London with 3 friends on the free tier, and said it was "So easy to split the budget amongst those to whom it applied." https://www.trustpilot.com/review/wanderlog.com
3. A family of six praised sharing: "the share feature is greatly appreciated by all my family members." https://www.trustpilot.com/review/wanderlog.com?page=2
4. Map plus search for places, travel times and auto-ordering of stops. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/wanderlog-travel-planner/id1476732439?see-all=reviews and https://tildes.net/~travel/1897/whats_your_planning_process_for_big_group_trips
5. Generous free tier. https://localsinsider.com/apps/wanderlog-review-free-offer-collaborative-travel-planning-done-right/

### Top complaints
1. **Friends will not log in.** A Feb 2024 reviewer said the friends on the trip "all don't want to log in the app" to see the daily plan, so they rebuilt the itinerary elsewhere. https://www.trustpilot.com/review/wanderlog.com?page=2
2. **Paid features help only the buyer.** An Oct 2025 reviewer said premium features "are only available for ourselves," so the rest of the group stays locked out; also called yearly-only billing shady. https://www.trustpilot.com/review/wanderlog.com?page=2
3. **Billing surprises.** Forced free trials, auto-renewal, a full year charged after a forgotten trial, a card pulled from Google Wallet. Seven such reviews on page 1. https://www.trustpilot.com/review/wanderlog.com
4. **Email import is unreliable.** "a little wonky" for hotels and not compatible with Airbnb (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/wanderlog-travel-planner/id1476732439?see-all=reviews); forwarded bookings "doesn't land in the trip," causing double bookings (Jul 2026, https://www.trustpilot.com/review/wanderlog.com).
5. **Slow, buggy, cluttered with many stops; wrong split math.** "it's so slow and buggy" (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/wanderlog.com); "Incorrect calculations for multiple trip mates" in the expense split (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/wanderlog.com?page=2); lags with many stops (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/wanderlog-travel-planner/id1476732439?see-all=reviews).
- Also: can't handle "branching" days when a big group splits up. https://tildes.net/~travel/1897/whats_your_planning_process_for_big_group_trips
- Also: exporting/sharing recommendations is paywalled. https://www.producthunt.com/products/wanderlog/reviews

## 2. TripIt (SAP Concur)

### Group collaboration
- Private invitation by email with three roles: "Can view," "Can edit," "Can edit and is traveling." Confirmation numbers, total cost and documents are hidden from view-only people and from public links. https://help.tripit.com/en/support/solutions/articles/103000063367 and https://help.tripit.com/en/support/solutions/articles/103000063347-trip-sharing-in-the-tripit-app
- Read-only "Send" by text or email does not add the trip to the recipient's account. Same page as above. Whether an editor needs a TripIt account is not stated on the help pages opened.
- Inner Circle (Pro) auto-shares plans with chosen people. https://www.tripit.com/web/pro
- No voting or suggestions. TripIt is a booking organizer, not a planner; a roundup calls its collaboration "Share-only." https://travelvient.com/guides/best-group-travel-planning-apps/

### Bookings import
- Free: forward to plans@tripit.com; Inbox Sync "adds plans from your inbox for you." https://www.tripit.com/web/free
- TripIt's own group-travel post pitches it as: forward bookings, then share the itinerary with the group. It pairs TripIt with Splitwise and Venmo for money. https://www.tripit.com/web/blog/travel-tips/best-group-travel-planning-app

### Costs
- No budgets or splitting. https://travelvient.com/guides/best-group-travel-planning-apps/

### Pricing
- TripIt Pro is $49 a year with a 30-day free trial. https://www.tripit.com/web/pro

### Praises
1. Forwarded emails import "most airline tickets, train tickets, and hotel reservations." https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tripit-travel-planner/id311035142?see-all=reviews
2. Free tier is useful on its own; a flight-cancellation alert "saved my holiday." Same page.
3. Huge installed base: 4.8 from 314K ratings. Same page.

### Complaints
1. **Sharing costs the other person.** Reviewers wish they could share "without them having to pay." https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tripit-travel-planner/id311035142?see-all=reviews; a Mar 2025 reviewer: "The trip sharing functionality is non-functional." https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.tripit.com
2. **Parser errors.** "a 5 day tour was listed as one hour!" (Jun 2026); four of eight forwarded emails had "wrong dates, wrong flight numbers and wrong times" (Oct 2025). https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.tripit.com
3. **Renewal and refund complaints.** Annual plan "Renewed ... without so much as a warning"; charges "non-refundable." Same page.
4. **Pro not worth it.** "not worth the price" (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tripit-travel-planner/id311035142?see-all=reviews); "Stick with the "free" version" (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.tripit.com).

## 3. Lambus

### Group collaboration
- Shared itinerary on a map; each person researches a thing or two they want to do. Invite flow, account rules and voting are not described. https://lambus.com/solutions/group

### Bookings import
- Forward confirmation emails; also .gpx route import. https://lambus.com/solutions/group and https://apps.apple.com/app/id1446178879

### Costs and splitting
- Group expenses with automatic who-owes-whom and direct payment ("We'll even do the math for you!"). https://apps.apple.com/app/id1446178879
- Excel export of who owes what is PRO. https://lambus.com/solutions/group

### Pricing (verified)
- US App Store: $3.99 a month, $24.49 a year, **$8.99 per trip**, $89.99 lifetime. https://apps.apple.com/app/id1446178879
- **One upgrade covers the whole trip**: "everyone can use all features of Lambus PRO without any additional costs." https://support.lambus.com/en/article/what-s-the-price-for-a-lambus-pro-upgrade
- PRO has 15 features incl. offline, calendar sync, chatbot, currency conversion, PDF export. https://support.lambus.com/en/articles/4941701-about-lambus-pro

### Praises and complaints (thin evidence: 83 ratings, 4.7)
- Praise: clean UI "easy to navigate"; group organization concept. https://apps.apple.com/app/id1446178879
- Complaints: place search returns same-named places worldwide; ChatGPT feature flaky and support slow to reply; early versions were organized by place, not time. Same page.
- Roundup: smaller user base, weak AI and discovery. https://travelvient.com/guides/best-group-travel-planning-apps/

## 4. Short notes

**Troupe (JetBlue Travel Products).** Built for deciding before booking: vote on dates, destinations, places to stay and things to do in one place instead of scattered texts. https://yourmileagemayvary.com/2022/10/22/jetblues-new-app-to-help-plan-group-trips-isnt-half-bad/ and https://www.popsci.com/diy/group-trip-planning-apps/ (Apr 2023; iOS only, free, no itinerary). Its App Store pages returned 404 when opened today, so its current status is unconfirmed. (A search summary, not opened, reported 3.9 stars, a broken invite link and no updates in a year.)

**Mindtrip (AI planner).** Free on iOS, 4.7 from 883 ratings; "invite friends or family to plan with you," group chat with the AI in the trip, booking inside the app, and import of places from videos, articles or Maps pins. No subscription listed. Complaints: slow or failed chat replies, filters resetting. https://apps.apple.com/app/id6503107567

**What big groups really do.** A Tildes thread on a 15-person trip: ideas go in a private Facebook group, people vote with likes, decided items go in a spreadsheet; it "works" but "doesn't feel great." Commenters say plan anchor meetups and let people branch off. https://tildes.net/~travel/1897/whats_your_planning_process_for_big_group_trips

Common advice in roundups is to use three apps: Troupe to decide, Wanderlog for the itinerary, Splitwise for money. https://travelvient.com/guides/best-group-travel-planning-apps/ and https://www.tripit.com/web/blog/travel-tips/best-group-travel-planning-app

## 5. What reviewers value that TripTab lacks

- **A map and place discovery** (Wanderlog, Lambus, Mindtrip): search, pins, travel times, route order. TripTab is a timeline with no map.
- **Offline itinerary** (Wanderlog free offline, Lambus PRO). People ask for "downloadable and printable." https://apps.apple.com/us/app/wanderlog-travel-planner/id1476732439?see-all=reviews
- **Native apps and alerts** (TripIt flight alerts, Wanderlog app). TripTab is a web app.
- **Gmail auto-scan** (Wanderlog Pro, TripIt Inbox Sync). TripTab asks for a forward, paste or photo.
- **Group voting** (Troupe; Facebook likes in the Tildes thread). TripTab has a Suggested state but, as described, no votes.
- **Scale and trust** (TripIt 314K ratings, Wanderlog 37K).

## 6. Where TripTab is clearly different

- **Settle-up is a side effect of planning.** Confirming a plan logs who paid and who joined. In Wanderlog and Lambus, expenses are a separate tab that someone has to fill in, and TripIt has none.
- **Per-plan joiners.** Splitting by who actually joined each plan handles the "branching" days Wanderlog cannot (Tildes thread).
- **AI import from many inputs** (paste, PDF, photo, forward to the trip's own address), not just email.
- **Plan status Suggested, Planned, Confirmed**, which no competitor reviewed shows.
- **Never moves money**, same as Wanderlog and Splitwise; Lambus offers direct payment.

## 7. Opportunities for TripTab (from the evidence)

1. **Join with no account and no app.** The group link opens the trip and the friend taps their name; sign-in comes later, only for edits that need it. Evidence: friends "all don't want to log in" (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/wanderlog.com?page=2); TripIt sharing that makes the other person pay (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tripit-travel-planner/id311035142?see-all=reviews). This hits TripTab's riskiest assumption (0 accepted invites in the spring) head on.
2. **Make the link useful before anyone joins.** Each friend's first view is "you owe $X, you're in 3 plans," with a one-tap "I'm in" on each Suggested plan. A balance about you is a reason to open the link; an itinerary alone is not. Evidence: people pair a planner with Splitwise just for money (https://www.tripit.com/web/blog/travel-tips/best-group-travel-planning-app); Wanderlog's split is "basic" or wrong (https://localsinsider.com/apps/wanderlog-review-free-offer-collaborative-travel-planning-done-right/, https://www.trustpilot.com/review/wanderlog.com?page=2).
3. **Vote on Suggested plans.** Thumbs or "I'm in" counts on Suggested cards, so the group chat's ideas and likes move into the trip. Evidence: Troupe's whole pitch (https://yourmileagemayvary.com/2022/10/22/jetblues-new-app-to-help-plan-group-trips-isnt-half-bad/); Facebook-likes workaround (https://tildes.net/~travel/1897/whats_your_planning_process_for_big_group_trips).
4. **Import that confirms it landed.** After a forward, reply by email with what was added and on which day, and flag anything unsure for review. Evidence: Wanderlog "doesn't land in the trip" (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/wanderlog.com); TripIt "wrong dates, wrong flight numbers" (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.tripit.com). Reliable import is the gap both leaders leave open.
5. **Lead with split days.** Show "who's going" per plan and the costs that follow from it (half the group skis, half goes to the spa), and say it in onboarding. Evidence: Wanderlog can't branch (https://tildes.net/~travel/1897/whats_your_planning_process_for_big_group_trips).
6. **An offline, printable trip.** Cache the trip and offer a one-page print or PDF for the mountain with no signal. Evidence: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/wanderlog-travel-planner/id1476732439?see-all=reviews; offline is the most common Pro feature.
7. **Honest pricing, if it charges.** A one-time trip pass that covers the whole group, no forced trial, no auto-renewal. Evidence: Lambus's $8.99 per trip and its rule that one upgrade covers everyone (https://apps.apple.com/app/id1446178879, https://support.lambus.com/en/article/what-s-the-price-for-a-lambus-pro-upgrade); Wanderlog and TripIt complaints about renewal and buyer-only features (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/wanderlog.com, https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.tripit.com).
8. **Payment handoff without moving money.** A "pay Sam on Venmo" link next to each balance. Evidence: a Wanderlog reviewer asked to "Integrate with Venmo/Stripe" (https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1476732439/wanderlog-travel-planner/reviews); TripIt's own group guide sends people to Venmo (https://www.tripit.com/web/blog/travel-tips/best-group-travel-planning-app).

## 8. What users say they would pay for (for the Sponsor Memo)

- **Market prices:** Wanderlog Pro is $39.99 a year (vendor post), with App Store tiers from $31.99 to $59.99 a year and $5.99 to $16.99 a month. TripIt Pro is $49 a year. Lambus is $3.99 a month, $24.49 a year, $8.99 a trip or $89.99 for life.
- **What paying users pay for:** offline maps, Gmail scanning, export to Maps or PDF, sorting and routing, flight alerts. One Wanderlog reviewer upgraded "for the sorting option." https://apps.apple.com/us/app/wanderlog-travel-planner/id1476732439?see-all=reviews
- **What blocks payment:** annual-only plans for people who travel once or twice a year (TripIt Pro "steep" for infrequent travelers, https://travelvient.com/guides/best-group-travel-planning-apps/); Pro that helps only the buyer; renewal surprises; paying just to share with someone (https://www.producthunt.com/products/wanderlog/reviews).
- **Inference (not a user quote):** For a 7 to 10 person ski trip, the best fit is a per-trip pass bought by the organizer that covers everyone, priced near Lambus's $8.99. Under $1 a person reads as a rounding error next to a ski house. This needs testing with real users; no source here shows anyone paying for a TripTab-like product.
