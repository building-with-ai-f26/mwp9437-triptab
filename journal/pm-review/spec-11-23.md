# Build spec: Mike's 11/23 ski trip test

Written 11/24 by Claude as product manager, from Mike's own notes on setting up the real ski trip on triptab.org the evening of 11/23. This file is the checklist a build session works from and checks its work against. It is the durable list: the runbook's Steps row points here, and calls Mike hasn't answered stay in the runbook's Open calls, not here. C58 to C61 were decided 11/24 and are written into the items below.

## How a build session uses this file

1. Read this file, then the runbook's Decided calls (C58 to C61, 11/24). Build in wave order. Finish and verify one item before starting the next.
2. An item marked **Waits on Cnn** is not built until that call is in Decided calls. If Mike's answer differs from the recommendation written here, rewrite the item's Behavior and Checks here first, in the same pull request.
3. An item is done only when every check under it is ticked, each with its evidence: a test name, a click-through screenshot at 1280 and 390, or an eval result file in `journal/evals/`. Then fill its row in the Completion table at the bottom.
4. Nothing here rewrites or deletes production data. Where a fix needs that, it is listed under Needs Mike's OK and waits.
5. Before the last merge, have a fresh agent read only this file and the diff (not the build session's summary) and list any check that isn't met. Fix what it finds.
6. Fixtures, evals and screenshots use made-up trips, names, amounts and booking codes. Never commit the real booking emails, codes, amounts or friends' names.

### T2. Own-pay plans: your own price and your own status

**Decided by Mike, C61 and 11/24 in chat:**
- Don't make people add a price. Someone who joins a plan where everyone pays their own only needs the ability to add theirs, and must never get the price Mike paid.
- The status is per person: Mike, who booked, sees Confirmed; a friend who joined but hasn't booked sees Planned.

**What the code does today.**
- [Code] The card's price on an own-pay plan is only your own line (`app/src/pages/TripPage.tsx:440-443`), and each person can log their own cost in the plan's panel (`app/src/components/trip/PlanPanel.tsx:353`, `log_own_line`). So the price part already works.
- [DB] The friend who joined both flights on 11/23 has no price on either; the only expense on each is Mike's. Nothing was copied.
- [Code] The status is one for the whole group: an own-pay plan reads Planned, "1 of 2 booked", until everyone going has booked (`TripPage.tsx:452-461`, `ownProgress`). That is why Mike's overnight flight went from Confirmed to Planned for him when the friend joined. This is what changes.

**Behavior.**
- No prompt after Join; adding your price stays optional. Mark as booked works with the price left blank (it starts blank; the plan's estimate is only a hint), and a booking with no price shows no "$0".
- On a plan where everyone pays their own, the status you see is your own: Confirmed once you have booked (marked yourself booked or logged your cost), Planned until then. A Suggested plan stays Suggested for everyone.
- Everywhere the status shows follows the same rule for the person looking: the card's look (solid card and stripe, the green "$ ✓" when your own cost is paid), Your plans, the plan's panel, the week view and the calendar export.
- The booked count ("1 of 2 booked") still shows beside it, so everyone sees who hasn't booked yet.
- Dropping yourself from an own-pay plan takes your own line with it (only yours); an Owner taking someone else out leaves that person's line alone.
- A plan nobody has logged a line on keeps its stored status, so an Owner's hand Confirm (C21, C26, C27) stays as it was.
- Split-bill plans keep one status for the whole group, as now.
- Prefer working out each person's status in the app from what is stored (who booked, own cost lines) over storing a status per person. If a migration turns out to be needed, it is additive and runs on TripTab dev first.

**Checks.**
- [x] Unit test of the per-person status: booked gives Confirmed; joined but not booked gives Planned; a Suggested plan stays Suggested for both; a split-bill plan is unchanged. Evidence: `statusFor` in `app/src/lib/ledger.ts`, tests "each person's own status on own-pay plans (T2)" in `ledger.test.ts` (7 tests).
- [x] Click-through on TripTab dev with two accounts, at 1280 and 390: A logs a price on an own-pay flight and books it; B joins. A sees Confirmed with A's price; B sees Planned with no price (not A's); both see "1 of 2 booked". Evidence: `e2e/own-status-flow.cjs`, `docs/screenshots/own-status-*`.
- [x] B marks themselves booked, with or without a price: B now sees Confirmed, and A's view doesn't change. Evidence: the same flow (B books with no price).
- [x] B adds their own price from the plan's panel; A's price stays as it was. Evidence: the same flow.
- [x] Your plans and the week view show the same status as the card for each person. Evidence: the same flow reads all three for both people at each step.
- [x] B taps Drop: only B's line (if any) goes, and A still sees Confirmed. Evidence: the same flow. `who-pays-flow` now expects "Confirmed for you" for the booker.

### T6. Repeat a plan on several days as separate plans

Decided by Mike on 11/23 and restated 11/24: the point is adding a plan to several days in one go (say, five ski days at once), and each day's plan is then its own. It replaces the 11/17 view on the Product Vision tab that one plan covering every day "works well".

**Problem.**
- [Mike, screenshot] A week of skiing is one plan, "Day 1 of 7", and only the first card carries the details.
- Mike wants a placeholder ski day on each day now, then to set each day's mountain later.

**Behavior.**
- In Add, More days lets you pick any set of days, not only days in a row (Mon, Wed and Sat, say): a chip per trip day to tick on or off, plus Every day of a stop as a shortcut. [Code] Today it only takes a run of days in a row (Mike, 11/24). Saving makes one plan per day picked, each with all the details (title, where, parts, times, status, cost settings), and each edited or deleted on its own.
- The new plans are not linked: there is no series and no "change all". Each is a plain plan, as if it had been added on its own.
- If a cost is entered, each day gets it (per day) unless the user picks one total.
- An existing multi-day activity or meal gets a Split into separate days button in its dialog. The user runs it; there is no migration.
- Flights, transport and stays still span, because each is one booking.

**Checks.**
- [ ] Unit test: seven days picked gives seven plans with the same fields on seven days, and three days not in a row (days 1, 3 and 6) give three plans on exactly those days.
- [ ] Click-through at 1280 and 390: making a ski day over seven days gives seven full cards. Changing day 3's place leaves the others alone. Deleting day 5 leaves six. Picking three days not in a row gives cards on just those three days.
- [ ] Split on an existing seven-day plan gives seven plans; the original goes; who's in and opt-ins carry over.
- [ ] No schema change is needed; if one turns out to be, it is additive, runs on TripTab dev first, and the RLS proof still passes.

### T7. A payment schedule on a plan (deposits and installments)

**Problem.**
- [Mike] The lodging is paid in two installments and only the first is paid. This is common, and TripTab should handle it.
- [DB] The lodging is one expense marked paid in full by Mike.
- [Mike, screenshot] The booking shows a payment schedule: two rows, each with a date and amount, the first ticked.

**Behavior.**
- A plan's cost can have several payment rows, each with an amount, currency, due date, paid or not, and who pays. The plan's cost is their sum.
- Each row splits the way the plan does.
- The card's money mark is the green "$ ✓" only when every row is paid. Until then it reads "1 of 2 paid · next due Dec 11".
- Each unpaid row with a due date makes a to-do, "Pay <plan>: payment 2", for its payer, due that day. Marking the row paid ticks the to-do, and ticking the to-do marks the row paid.
- In Money, paid rows count as spent and unpaid rows as coming up. A foreign row uses the day's rate on the day it is paid.
- Import: when a confirmation shows a payment schedule, propose the rows. That is a prompt change, so it waits on the eval check below.
- Prefer extending `expenses` (already linked to a plan by `item_id`) with a due date and a paid flag over a new table. The migration is additive.

**Checks.**
- [ ] Unit tests: cents add up exactly across rows and splits; each foreign row is converted on its own date.
- [ ] Click-through at 1280 and 390: a stay of 3,000 EUR as 1,000 paid on 11/18 and 2,000 due 12/11 shows "1 of 2 paid", and a to-do for the payer due 12/11 appears. Marking it paid shows "$ ✓" and ticks the to-do.
- [ ] Money shows 1,000 EUR (converted) as spent and 2,000 EUR as coming up.
- [ ] Plans with one expense look exactly as before.
- [ ] Import eval: a new made-up case with a two-row schedule passes, and the other cases don't drop.
- [ ] The RLS proof passes.

### T10. Ask AI: a chat that offers a few options

**Decided by Mike, C59 and C60 (11/24): yes.** This brings back the chat parked until after the course, as a panel, not a floating bubble.

**Problem.**
- [Mike] Asking Plan with AI for a fondue dinner gave one option. He wants a few, each with the lowdown, then to choose which, when and what time. If it needs a reservation, that should go on the to-do list.
- [DB] That call returned 1 idea in 6.6 seconds; the five minutes before Add was Mike deciding, not waiting.
- [Code] `supabase/functions/plan-with-ai` fills empty slots with one idea per slot.

**Behavior.**
- A chat panel. Each ask returns three options, each with: name, why it fits, price level, the best time, whether it needs a reservation, and a source link.
- Pick one: the day and part are filled in and can be changed, and it is added as Suggested. Follow-ups refine the options ("cheaper", "closer to the chalet").
- If an option needs a reservation, offer "Add Book <place> to To do", with an assignee and due date.
- The chat is private to the person who asked (C60). What they pick lands on the trip as Suggested, where everyone sees it and can vote.
- The AI rules hold:
  - It runs in an Edge Function, behind the Owner check, on Claude Haiku 5.5 at low effort, with a web search cap.
  - Each turn counts toward the 20 calls a person a day and `AI_DAILY_CAP_USD`.
  - Each turn is logged in `ai_calls` (new feature name `ask`).
  - It never invents a place, never overwrites a plan, and every option has its source.

**Checks.**
- [ ] A 10-case eval (made-up trips), in `app/evals/ask/`, run before Mike sees it. Each reply has three options, each with a source, each in the right city and fitting the ask, with the reservation flag right on the obvious cases, and none duplicating a plan already on the trip. Record the cost per turn in `journal/evals/README.md`.
- [ ] Click-through with a canned reply at 1280 and 390, then one live run.
- [ ] A reservation becomes a to-do with the chosen assignee.
- [ ] The chat is stored per person under RLS: another account, even an Owner of the same trip, can't read it (add the case to `e2e/rls-proof.cjs`).
