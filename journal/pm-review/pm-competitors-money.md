# TripTab competitor review: the expense-splitting side

Read on 11/23/2026. Apps covered: Splitwise (required), Tricount, Settle Up and Splid. I picked these three because each one comes up again and again in Splitwise complaints as the app people moved to, and because each has a trip-focused model worth comparing (Tricount: free with a bank behind it; Settle Up: per-trip group passes; Splid: a one-time unlock with no sign-up).

**How this was sourced.** Every claim below links to a page I opened in this session. Google Play reviews were read from the live listing pages (the "most helpful" reviews Google shows on each app page, not the full review list). App Store reviews are the handful Apple shows on the listing page. **Reddit could not be reached**: both the fetch tool and the search tool refused reddit.com, so there is no Reddit evidence here. Trustpilot for Settle Up returned 404. Two old Tricount help pages (login and opening links on the web) also returned 404, so I left their claims out. Anything not verified is marked **(unverified)**.

---

## 1. Splitwise

### What it does for a group trip
- Groups for trips, houses and so on; expenses split equally, by percent, shares or exact amounts; "simplify debts" into the fewest payments; CSV export; comments on expenses; works on Android, iOS and web. The Play listing names "Splitting a vacation house for skiing" as a use case. [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.Splitwise.SplitwiseMobile&hl=en_US&gl=US)
- Settle up through Venmo or PayPal (US, USD only): Splitwise hands you off to the Venmo or PayPal app with the recipient prefilled. As of 7/2/26 the Venmo hand-off does not work in the Android app "due to a change on Venmo's end." [Splitwise help: send money via PayPal or Venmo](https://kb.splitwise.com/payment-integrations/how-do-i-send-money-via-paypal-or-venmo)
- New in October 2026: **Itemize**, free in the US and UK. One person photographs a restaurant receipt, everyone taps what they ordered, tax and tip are shared out, and friends can join that one split by scanning a QR code "without downloading anything or creating an account." [Digital Trends, 10/14/2026](https://digitaltrends.com/phones/splitwise-now-lets-you-calculate-everyones-share-of-a-restaurant-bill-including-tax-and-tip/), [The Paypers, 10/16/2026](https://thepaypers.com/payments/news/splitwise-launches-itemize-receipt-splitting-feature-in-the-us-and-uk)

### How a new member joins
- You can't join a group on your own. A member adds your email or phone, or sends an "Invite via link" from group settings; if the group doesn't show up, you open the link and log into your account. [Splitwise help: join an existing group](https://kb.splitwise.com/groups/how-do-i-join-an-existing-group)
- You can add someone by name only as a placeholder, but only from the website, and Splitwise warns against fake emails. [Splitwise help: add a friend without email or phone](https://kb.splitwise.com/getting-started/can-i-add-a-friend-without-adding-their-email-address-or-phone-number)
- So for a normal group (outside the one-off Itemize QR split), each person ends up with a Splitwise account to see and add expenses. A long-standing App Store review asks for exactly this to change: "I wish splitwise allowed group splitting w/o requiring everyone to signup" (14-person family trip). [App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/splitwise/id458023433?see-all=reviews)

### Pricing and the paywall
- **Free plan: up to 4 expenses a day**, stated by Splitwise itself. Pro: "Add as many expenses as you need without hitting a limit." [Splitwise help: what is Splitwise Pro](https://kb.splitwise.com/pro/what-is-splitwise-pro)
- Free also means ads, and reviewers describe a countdown before adding an expense. A 12/30/2023 news write-up reports the cap arriving with a 10-second cooldown. [ITVoice, 12/30/2023](https://www.itvoice.in/splitwise-has-introduced-restrictions-on-the-number-of-free-expenses-users-can-add), [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/splitwise.com)
- **Behind Pro:** unlimited expenses, card transaction import (US), search, currency conversion, receipt scanning and itemizing (on the Pro page), charts, default splits, no ads, early access. [Splitwise Pro page](https://www.splitwise.com/pro), [help page](https://kb.splitwise.com/pro/what-is-splitwise-pro)
- **Plans:** Individual + Trip Pass (annual), Duo + Trip Pass (annual, two people), Monthly. A yearly plan includes one **Trip Pass** that gives every member of one trip group Pro for 30 days. [Splitwise help: what is Splitwise Pro](https://kb.splitwise.com/pro/what-is-splitwise-pro)
- **Price:** Splitwise's own pages show no price. The US App Store lists Pro purchases from $2.99 to $4.99 a month and $29.99 or $39.99 a year, without saying which is which. [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/splitwise/id458023433). A Play reviewer says Pro went "from $3 to $5 a month" (Feb 2024). [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.Splitwise.SplitwiseMobile&hl=en_US&gl=US). The commonly quoted $4.99/month and $39.99/year comes from a competitor's blog, so treat it as approximate. [Finny blog, 8/6/2026](https://getfinny.app/blog/splitwise-pricing-2026)
- Ratings: 4.0 on the US App Store (30K ratings); 3.9 on Google Play (about 195K reviews, 10M+ downloads); 2.0 on Trustpilot (72 reviews, 64% one-star). [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/splitwise/id458023433), [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.Splitwise.SplitwiseMobile&hl=en_US&gl=US), [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/splitwise.com)

### What users praise
1. **Built for group trips.** "Great for splitting expenses with a travel group"; "Super usefull for group trips" (4 stars even with the cap). [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/splitwise.com)
2. **Simple and focused.** "simple and focused"; "straightforward and easy to use." [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/splitwise.com)
3. **Flexible splits and itemizing.** Shares, percentages and item-by-item receipts; one roommate reviewer calls Pro "100% worth it." [App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/splitwise/id458023433?see-all=reviews)
4. **Takes the stress out of tracking debts**, used for years by long-time fans (several of the angry reviews open with "used to be great" or ten years of use). [App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/splitwise/id458023433?see-all=reviews), [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.Splitwise.SplitwiseMobile&hl=en_US&gl=US)

### What users complain about
1. **The daily cap, ads and countdown (by far the top theme).** "Can't add more than 2 expenses a day" (Aug 2026), annoying Pro prompts, the annual fee called "overkill." [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/splitwise.com). "limiting the number of transactions you can add is not a good look" (Jan 2024); "Delays and ads between adding expenses" (Apr 2024). [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.Splitwise.SplitwiseMobile&hl=en_US&gl=US). A reviewer who used it for group trips: "Recently they limited the amount of transactions you can input a day to just 4!" and is going back to spreadsheets. [App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/splitwise/id458023433?see-all=reviews)
2. **Useful features moved behind Pro**, including currency conversion: "they paywalled nearly everything useful"; another notes multi-currency needs Pro. Friends left because of the cost. [App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/splitwise/id458023433?see-all=reviews)
3. **Payments go wrong.** Splitwise Pay money stuck "in a 'pending' state for days" after a five-person trip; no Zelle; Venmo linking broke. [App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/splitwise/id458023433?see-all=reviews). Support called "nonexistent," no phone number. [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/splitwise.com)
4. **Hard to trust or control.** Split options called confusing, reconciliations questioned, and any group member can edit or delete expenses. [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/splitwise.com)
5. **Everyone must sign up** (see joining above). [App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/splitwise/id458023433?see-all=reviews)

### What Splitwise does that TripTab doesn't, and reviewers value
- **Free receipt itemizing with QR guest join** (Oct 2026), the one feature that lets a non-user into a split with no account. [Digital Trends](https://digitaltrends.com/phones/splitwise-now-lets-you-calculate-everyones-share-of-a-restaurant-bill-including-tax-and-tip/). TripTab lists receipt scanning as "not started, on purpose."
- **Name recognition and a 10M+ install base**: the group is often already on it. A competitor guide notes paying for one month of Pro is often easier than moving a group mid-trip. [Finny blog](https://getfinny.app/blog/splitwise-pricing-2026) (competitor source)
- Card transaction import (Pro, US) and spending charts. [Splitwise Pro page](https://www.splitwise.com/pro)

## 2. Tricount (owned by bunq, a Dutch bank)

### What it does and how people join
- Trips, homes, couples and events; split equally, by amount or custom shares; multiple currencies; works offline; "100% free with no ads or limits"; 17 million users claimed. [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tribab.tricount.android&hl=en_US&gl=US), [tricount.com](https://tricount.com/). (The Play page header also shows a "Contains ads" label, which contradicts the description; I can't tell which is current.)
- Can import a Splitwise group. [tricount.com](https://tricount.com/)
- **Joining now happens in the app:** open the app, Add tricount, Join an existing tricount, paste the link, Join; without the app the link sends you to the App Store or Google Play. [Tricount: join](https://tricount.com/link/join). Release notes say the join flow explains "when and why login is needed." [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tricount/id349866256). A July 2026 reviewer calls it "impossible to use it without downloading an app" and "a horrible update." [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tricount.com)
- One Play reviewer finds it confusing that there's no "Invite" button after a group is made, only "Share." [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tribab.tricount.android&hl=en_US&gl=US)

### Pricing
- Free, no subscription. **Tricount Premium was discontinued** with the new app; former subscribers were offered six months of bunq Pro (€59.94). Features removed: CSV and PDF export, personal mode; statistics and custom splits "planned for a future update." [Tricount help, 11/16/2026](https://help.tricount.com/articles/what-happened-with-tricount-premium)
- The business model is the bank: a bunq card that adds shared expenses automatically as you spend. [tricount.com](https://tricount.com/)
- Ratings: 4.9 on the US App Store (7.7K); 4.6 on Google Play (about 197K reviews, 10M+ downloads); 3.4 on Trustpilot (46 reviews). [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tricount/id349866256), [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tribab.tricount.android&hl=en_US&gl=US), [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tricount.com)

### Praise
1. **Free with no ads**, and a home for Splitwise refugees: one left over daily entry limits, another will "never use SplitWise again!" [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tricount.com)
2. **Easy to add and split entries.** "love the ease with which you can add entries" (Nov 2026); "Free version less limited than similar apps. Great UX." [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tribab.tricount.android&hl=en_US&gl=US)
3. **Multiple currencies.** "Great app, even with different currencies." [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tricount.com)
4. **Simplest way to share costs**; redesign praised. [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tricount.com)

### Complaints
1. **Wrong balances.** Nov 19, 2026: both members suddenly shown owing each other "extremely large" sums, a "Critical calculation bug"; bunq replied it is "aware of an issue affecting balances." [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tribab.tricount.android&hl=en_US&gl=US). Balances "were wrong" when checked against a spreadsheet (May 2026). [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tricount.com)
2. **Export removed.** Users asking for Excel export; bunq says export is "no longer in the app" and offers a CSV by email. [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tribab.tricount.android&hl=en_US&gl=US). "not having an export or big picture view ... is becoming a dealbreaker." [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tricount/id349866256)
3. **App-only access after the update** (see joining). [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tricount.com)
4. **Lost data.** A week-long trip erased after an update; Tricount said the data "wasn't uploaded to our servers." [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tricount/id349866256)
5. **Distrust of the bank owner**, called a data-harvesting app (the reviewer's claim, not verified). [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tricount.com)

### What it does that TripTab doesn't
- Offline entry, multiple currencies for free, and a card that logs shared spending automatically. [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tribab.tricount.android&hl=en_US&gl=US), [tricount.com](https://tricount.com/)

## 3. Settle Up (Step Up Labs)

### What it does and how people join
- Expenses with multiple payers, member weights (a couple counts as two), incomes, items from a long bill, all currencies at real-time rates, works offline, Android, iOS and **web**. **"doesn't need every group member to download the app"**; group sharing "via a link or QR code"; **read-only access** for some members; CSV export free. [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=cz.destil.settleup&hl=en_US&gl=US), [settleup.io](https://settleup.io/)
- The App Store listing says friends can view the group balance in a browser without installing the app; it doesn't say whether joining needs an account. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id737534985)

### Pricing (US App Store)
- Free core with ads. Premium: no ads, receipt photos, categories, recurring and future expenses, Excel export, charts. [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=cz.destil.settleup&hl=en_US&gl=US)
- Personal: $3.99/month or $19.99/year. **Group Premium, which covers the whole group: $5.99 for 1 week, $9.99 for 2 weeks, $14.99 for 1 month, $29.99 for 2 months, $39.99 for 1 year, $149.99 lifetime.** [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id737534985)
- Ratings: 4.8 on the US App Store (1.8K); 4.8 on Google Play (about 51K reviews, 1M+ downloads). [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id737534985), [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=cz.destil.settleup&hl=en_US&gl=US). Trustpilot page not found (404).

### Praise
1. **A trip companion.** "Fantastic travel companion for group expenses"; "a game changer on a recent group trip." [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id737534985)
2. **Fast entry.** "the fastest expense adding framework": tap the payer's name and go. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id737534985)
3. **Fair weights and currencies**, and the one-time group premium "can be reset for future trips" (reviewer's account). [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id737534985)
4. **Seen as better than Splitwise** ("better than Splitwise," Oct 2026). [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=cz.destil.settleup&hl=en_US&gl=US)

### Complaints
- Few complaints were visible on the listings I could open. One reviewer calls the design "somewhat dated" (paraphrased by the page summary), and another lists missing features (excluding fixed amounts from a split, adjusting one person's share, grouping payers by family) with no reply from the developers. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id737534985). I could not reach a fuller set of negative reviews, so this side is thin.

### What it does that TripTab doesn't
- Browser access for people without the app, read-only members, weights, multiple payers, offline, and **per-trip group pricing**. [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=cz.destil.settleup&hl=en_US&gl=US), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id737534985)

## 4. Splid

### What it does and how people join
- Works on and offline; turn on sync to enter expenses together; **"no sign-up required"**; more than 150 currencies with automatic conversion; multiple payers and unequal splits; fewest payments; PDF summary (Excel export is a paid unlock). [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=splid.teamturtle.com.splid&hl=en_US&gl=US)
- People join a shared group with a code (lengthened from six to nine characters). No web version: a 11/25/2025 reviewer wishes "it could have a web version." [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id991473495)

### Pricing
- One-time in-app purchases: Splid Plus $3.99 and "2 Groups" $2.99 (the listing doesn't say exactly what each unlocks). [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id991473495). Competitor pages say the free tier allows one group and Plus unlocks unlimited groups (**unverified**, competitor sources: [Kittysplit](https://www.kittysplit.com/en/splid-alternative)).
- Ratings: 4.9 on the US App Store (4.2K); 4.9 on Google Play (about 88K reviews, 1M+ downloads). [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id991473495), [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=splid.teamturtle.com.splid&hl=en_US&gl=US)

### Praise
1. **No subscription, a fair one-time price.** "Thank you for being a non greedy splitwise," happy to pay "a one off payment" (112 people found it helpful). [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=splid.teamturtle.com.splid&hl=en_US&gl=US)
2. **No daily cap.** A Splitwise switcher: "Splid doesn't limit you." [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id991473495)
3. **Works with bad cell service and foreign currencies** on a trip: "The currency calculator was a huge help." [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id991473495)
4. **Only what's needed, no social clutter**: "the best app to manage group expenses during a trip." [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=splid.teamturtle.com.splid&hl=en_US&gl=US)

### Complaints
1. **No web version** for bigger screens. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id991473495)
2. **No read-only members and no notes field** on expenses (feature requests). [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=splid.teamturtle.com.splid&hl=en_US&gl=US)
3. Few other complaints were visible on the listings; I could not reach a fuller negative set.

## Opportunities for TripTab

1. **Never cap or slow down entries, and say so on the front page.** The single loudest complaint about the market leader is the 4-a-day cap, ads and countdown, and it hits trips hardest because a trip day has many costs. Every alternative that wins switchers leads with "no limits." Sources: [Splitwise help](https://kb.splitwise.com/pro/what-is-splitwise-pro), [Splitwise Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/splitwise.com), [Splitwise Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.Splitwise.SplitwiseMobile&hl=en_US&gl=US), [Splitwise App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/splitwise/id458023433?see-all=reviews), [Tricount Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tribab.tricount.android&hl=en_US&gl=US), [Splid App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id991473495).

2. **Win the join step in the browser, and keep it to one tap.** This is the riskiest assumption, and the market is split: Splitwise needs an account for groups, Tricount just moved to app-only joining and drew "a horrible update," while Settle Up and Splid are praised for no download or no sign-up. Watch out: Splitwise's free Itemize now lets a guest into a single bill by QR with no account, so "join without an account" is no longer unique on its own. TripTab's edge has to be the whole trip opening from one link in the group chat, picking your name, with sign-in as light as possible. Worth measuring how many friends drop off at the sign-in step. Sources: [Splitwise join help](https://kb.splitwise.com/groups/how-do-i-join-an-existing-group), [Tricount join](https://tricount.com/link/join), [Tricount Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tricount.com), [Settle Up Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=cz.destil.settleup&hl=en_US&gl=US), [Splid Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=splid.teamturtle.com.splid&hl=en_US&gl=US), [Digital Trends on Itemize](https://digitaltrends.com/phones/splitwise-now-lets-you-calculate-everyones-share-of-a-restaurant-bill-including-tax-and-tip/).

3. **Make every balance traceable line by line.** Tricount has a live balance bug (11/19/2026) and spreadsheet mismatches; Splitwise users question reconciliations. Tying each cost to a confirmed plan lets TripTab show "you owe $84 because of these 3 plans," which is something the expense-only apps can't do. Sources: [Tricount Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tribab.tricount.android&hl=en_US&gl=US), [Tricount Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tricount.com), [Splitwise Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/splitwise.com).

4. **Survive bad signal on the mountain.** Splid and Settle Up are praised for working offline, one reviewer specifically for a trip with unreliable cell service, and Tricount lost a week of trip data that never reached its servers. At minimum, TripTab should never lose a cost typed while offline (queue it and show it as unsynced). Sources: [Splid App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id991473495), [Settle Up Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=cz.destil.settleup&hl=en_US&gl=US), [Tricount App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tricount/id349866256).

5. **Free multiple currencies for ski trips abroad.** Currency conversion is Pro-only in Splitwise and a complaint there, while Tricount, Splid and Settle Up are praised for it at no charge. The vision's own example trip is Chamonix, Barcelona and Andorra, so the stretch goal matters for the target user. Sources: [Splitwise help](https://kb.splitwise.com/pro/what-is-splitwise-pro), [Splitwise App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/splitwise/id458023433?see-all=reviews), [Tricount Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tricount.com), [Splid App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id991473495).

6. **Free export of the trip and the tab.** Tricount removed CSV and PDF export and users call it a dealbreaker; Splid charges for Excel; Settle Up keeps CSV free. TripTab's planned PDF export (Build 4) should include the settle-up and be free. Sources: [Tricount help](https://help.tricount.com/articles/what-happened-with-tricount-premium), [Tricount Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tribab.tricount.android&hl=en_US&gl=US), [Tricount App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tricount/id349866256), [Splid Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=splid.teamturtle.com.splid&hl=en_US&gl=US).

7. **Show who changed what.** Splitwise users dislike that any member can edit or delete expenses; Settle Up offers read-only members and a Splid user asks for them. TripTab already has Owner and Viewer; adding a visible change history on each cost would address the trust worry while keeping "everyone is an owner" on casual trips. Sources: [Splitwise Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/splitwise.com), [Settle Up Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=cz.destil.settleup&hl=en_US&gl=US), [Splid Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=splid.teamturtle.com.splid&hl=en_US&gl=US).

8. **Keep the payment hand-off simple and include Zelle.** Splitwise's Venmo hand-off is broken on Android since 7/2/26, its own Splitwise Pay leaves money "pending" for days, and a reviewer wants Zelle. TripTab never holding money is a strength: prefilled Venmo and Zelle links plus a "mark as paid" step, tested on both iPhone and Android. Sources: [Splitwise help](https://kb.splitwise.com/payment-integrations/how-do-i-send-money-via-paypal-or-venmo), [Splitwise App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/splitwise/id458023433?see-all=reviews).

## What people say they'd pay for (for the Sponsor Memo)

- **One-off or per-trip, not a monthly subscription.** The most-liked Splid review would happily pay "a one off payment" and calls subscriptions "greed." [Splid Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=splid.teamturtle.com.splid&hl=en_US&gl=US). A Splitwise reviewer would pay about £1 a month but finds the annual price too high. [Splitwise Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/splitwise.com)
- **The market already sells trip passes.** Settle Up's Group Premium covers the whole group for $5.99 a week, $9.99 for two weeks or $14.99 a month. [Settle Up App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id737534985). Splitwise bundles a 30-day Trip Pass for a whole trip group into its yearly plans. [Splitwise help](https://kb.splitwise.com/pro/what-is-splitwise-pro). Splid sells a $3.99 one-time unlock. [Splid App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id991473495). The vision's own research notes Lambus at $8.99 per trip.
- **Some do pay for extras.** A roommate reviewer calls Splitwise Pro "100% worth it" for item-level receipt splits. [Splitwise App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/splitwise/id458023433?see-all=reviews)
- **What people refuse to pay for:** basic entry. Capping entries drove users away and became the competitors' main pitch. [Splitwise Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.Splitwise.SplitwiseMobile&hl=en_US&gl=US)
- **Free is also a strong position:** Tricount gives everything away and makes money through a bank card. [tricount.com](https://tricount.com/)
- **Implication:** the evidence points to a free core (plans, costs, settle-up with no cap) and an optional **per-trip pass, about $5 to $10, paid once by one person for the whole group**, covering extras such as AI import, PDF export or currencies. Whether a ski group would actually pay is not shown here; that needs a real test.
