== Finding D's fix, simulated on TripTab dev: a Google sign-in linked to three test accounts ==
One statement that undid itself (it ends by raising its result, which rolls everything back). Each
account got a sign-in shaped like the one Supabase Auth writes when Google links.
BEFORE
  1 pending, Google same address:   password=yes sessions=1 sign-ins=email        forwarding=1
  2 pending, Google other address:  password=yes sessions=1 sign-ins=email        forwarding=0
  3 confirmed, Google same address: password=yes sessions=1 sign-ins=email        forwarding=0
AFTER
  1 pending, Google same address:   password=no  sessions=0 sign-ins=google       forwarding=0
  2 pending, Google other address:  password=yes sessions=1 sign-ins=email+google forwarding=0
  3 confirmed, Google same address: password=yes sessions=1 sign-ins=email+google forwarding=0
So the password, the earlier session and the forwarding address go only where Google vouches for
the very address TripTab was still waiting on (1); a different Google address (2) and an account
that had already confirmed its email (3) are left as they were.
Limit of this check: the rows were written by the database's own admin role, since it may not act
as Supabase Auth's role ("permission denied to set role supabase_auth_admin"). A real Google
sign-in on a Preview is still the end-to-end check.
